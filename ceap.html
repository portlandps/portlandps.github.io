Next Steps

Each Parent Completes
Parent Questionnaire
If more than one parent wants to complete this questionnaire, each parent should complete it separately, as it is not designed for parents to complete it together.

Complete Only Once
Information Form
This form is to be completed only once and by a parent.

Calendar Invites
I typically send calendar invites for appointments.
The first appointment is for parents only (without the client or other children) to gather a wide variety of information (e.g., developmental history) and discuss your goals for services openly. If the items above are not completed, we won’t be able to proceed with the first appointment.
The next appointment is with your child to hear your child’s perspective and discuss the goals your child is motivated to work toward.

Signing Link emails
Sometime before the first appointment, each parent will receive an email from notifications@zohosign.com with a link to sign the Informed Consent Agreement. If this can’t be signed before or at the beginning of the first appointment, we won’t be able to proceed with the first appointment.
